Privacy Policy

Glassy Tile Explorer · Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Who we are

Glassy Tile Explorer is a casual tile-matching game published by Naazonet ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Contact us at Naazonet.contact@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Game data on your device: your level progress, stars, coins, hearts, booster counts, daily-spin time, and settings (music/sound volume, background). This is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Automatically collected by services used in the game: advertising ID, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location based on IP address (country/region), crash and performance reports, and ad interaction events.
  • Purchase information: purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation of what was bought (item, time, order ID) but never your card or bank details.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example "hearts are full" or "free spin ready"). These are created locally and are not sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.
  • If you contact us: your email address and the content of your message.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone access, and the game does not require an account.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game, save and restore your progress, and deliver items you buy or earn.
  • To show rewarded video ads and measure ad performance.
  • To understand how the game is used, fix crashes, and improve gameplay.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating and abuse, and to respond to support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Advertising

Third-party ad networks (for example Google AdMob) may show ads in the game and may use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads and measure results. Rewarded video ads are optional; you choose whether to watch them. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation on Android in Settings > Google > Ads. Buying "Remove Ads" removes forced full-screen ads; optional rewarded offers may remain available.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the game (such as ad networks, analytics, crash reporting and Google Play), when required by law, or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell your personal information for money. Ad partners may receive device identifiers as described above.

6. Storage and security

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage; this will delete your progress, coins and boosters. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children

Glassy Tile Explorer is a general-audience game and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age set by the law of your country). Users under 18 should play with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact Naazonet.contact@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can reset your advertising ID, delete local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling, and contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you or to request correction or deletion. Depending on where you live (for example India, the EU/UK or California), you may have additional legal rights over your personal data, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version and its date are always available in the game under Settings, and on this page. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the changes.
